Debit ya Credit — Pehchano kaise?

Har item ko yaad karne ki zaroorat nahi. Bas ek chain of questions poochho, aur item khud bata dega ki wo Asset hai, Liability, Expense ya Income — aur usse Debit/Credit side mil jayegi.

Sabse pehla trick

"Ye cheez humare paas hai / humein milegi — ya humein dena hai / humne khoya hai?"

Paas hai / milega → Asset ya Expense → Debit

Dena hai / kamaya hai → Liability, Capital ya Income → Credit

4 Categories — ek nazar mein

Assets DEBIT

Firm ke paas jo cheez hai — Cash, Bank, Furniture, Debtors, Stock, Machinery.

Expenses / Losses DEBIT

Jo kharcha hua ya nuksan hua — Salary, Rent Paid, Purchases, Bad Debts, Discount Allowed.

Liabilities CREDIT

Firm ko jo dena hai — Creditors, Bank Overdraft, Loan Taken, Outstanding Expenses.

Income / Gains + Capital CREDIT

Jo kamaya ya owner ka investment — Sales, Discount Received, Rent Received, Capital.

Confuse ho jaye to — 3 sawal poochho

Sawal 1 — Ye cheez chhoo sakte ho ya bank/cash mein dikhti hai?
Haan → Ye ek Asset hai (jaise Furniture, Stock, Cash). Debit
Sawal 2 — Kya ye kisi ko chukana hai (future mein paisa dena hai)?
Haan → Ye ek Liability hai (jaise Creditors, Loan, Overdraft). Credit
Sawal 3 — Business chalane mein kharch hua, ya business se kamaya?
Kharch hua → Expense Debit  |  Kamaya → Income Credit
Special case yaad rakho: "Returns" hamesha apne opposite ka ulta hote hain — Purchase Return (Returns Outward) Credit hota hai kyunki ye Purchases ko kam karta hai; Sales Return (Returns Inward) Debit hota hai kyunki ye Sales ko kam karta hai.
